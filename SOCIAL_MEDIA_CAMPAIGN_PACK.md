## 📌 Executive Campaign Strategy & Target Demographics

KhLan is positioned as **Cambodia's First AI-Powered Trusted Automotive Marketplace & Dealership OS**. The marketing campaign directly tackles the three biggest pain points in the Cambodian used car market:
1. **Fear of Fraud & Hidden Damage**: Buyers terrified of cut-and-welded chassis (*ឡានកាត់ត*), rolled-back mileage, and flood damage.
2. **Payment & Listing Friction**: Sellers tired of waiting hours or days for admin approvals.
3. **Deposit Scams & Middleman Brokers**: Buyers losing money to fake deposit requests on unverified social channels.

### Campaign Pillars:
- **Pillar A: AI Vehicle Inspection & Valuation** *(Audience: Car Buyers & Enthusiasts)*
- **Pillar B: Instant Bakong KHQR & Bank Slip OCR** *(Audience: Private Sellers & Dealership Owners)*
- **Pillar C: Strict 2-Party Anti-Scam Direct Chat** *(Audience: Safety-Conscious Buyers & Verified Sellers)*

## 🛠️ PART 4: AI Generation Technical Guidelines & Settings

### For Video Generation (Runway Gen-3 / Kling):
1. **Aspect Ratio**: Always select `9:16` for TikTok/Reels, or `16:9` for horizontal desktop/billboard ads.
2. **Motion Slider**: Keep motion value between `4` and `6` to ensure realistic vehicle reflections and smooth camera gimbal panning.
3. **Seed Consistency**: Use consistent seed numbers when rendering consecutive scenes featuring the same vehicle model.

### For Image Generation (Midjourney v6.1):
1. **Photorealism Flags**: Use `--v 6.1 --style raw --q 2`.
2. **Lighting Keyphrases**: `"commercial automotive studio lighting"`, `"cinematic volumetric backlight"`, `"Hasselblad 8k"`.
3. **Text Avoidance**: For clean UI elements, let Midjourney generate the blank HUD glass, then composite crisp typography in Figma or Canva.
